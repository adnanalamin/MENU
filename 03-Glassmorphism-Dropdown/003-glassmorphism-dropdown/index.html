Get Started

Navigation that lets the light through.

Open any menu above. Every dropdown works with a mouse, a keyboard and a touch screen, and the glass shows whatever sits behind it.

Mouse
Hover or click a menu to open it. Click anywhere else to close it.
Keyboard
Press Enter or Space to open, the arrow keys to move through items, and Escape to close.
Touch
On narrow screens the bar becomes a hamburger menu with accordion sections.

About

NOVA is a demo brand for a self-contained UI component: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no frameworks and no build step. Switch the theme with the button in the bar; your choice is saved in this browser.
